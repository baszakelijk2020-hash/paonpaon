The Residents Club®

The AM House Residents Club™ is envisioned as the private members’ club of Atelier Munro.

 

Think Soho House, Casa Cipriani, or

AP House - but here, your Atelier Munro wardrobe is part of the welcome.

WHALE CLUB Hong Kong — Initial $ 50.000, then $ 1.500 per month

The Residents Club® combines the calm of a first-class airport lounge with the sophistication of a luxury hotel bar.

The Residents Club® EVENTS

The beating heart of The Residents Club® is a members-only calendar of exclusive experiences and activities.

There are two types of calendars:

1.

Member-led calendar

The Residents Club® member community will naturally comprise of individuals from a wide variety of companies within different industries, each bringing their own unique connections, expertise and access.

Members will be encouraged to submit activities and experiences to The Residents Club® calendar committee, creating a dynamic agenda where members of the members' club might find themselves visiting:

the ABN AMRO trading floor;

the Feadship shipyard;

or on the roof of the Movenpick Hotel during Sail Amsterdam.

This calendar, led by The Residents Club®, also includes monthly business, networking, and professional events that would feature headlining, high-profile guests and speakers such as corporate leaders, entrepreneurs, athletes, and artists.

The Residents Club® offers ambitious individuals a fresh new way to connect and collaborate, providing a modern alternative to traditional business clubs and networking formats.

Crucially, The Residents Club® creates the environment and opportunities for customers to wear their finest Atelier Munro pieces.

High quality cooking, worth a stop.

Excellent cooking, worth a detour.

Exceptional cuisine, worth a special journey.

Much like the Michelin Guide was originally created to get people on the road, to drive to restaurants - and in turn use more tires - The Residents Club® creates occasions that effectively give customers more reasons to care about their attire, which in turn justifies the investment they make in it.

The aim is for customers to re-evaluate how they spend their disposable income by placing them in a context where they are surrounded by new peers, who are all impeccably dressed in Atelier Munro, rather than among their existing circles where that need may not exist.

In the Netherlands, concepts like these are rare. While Soho House on Spuistraat caters to a creative audience, The Residents Club® targets a broader entrepreneurial and corporate demographic.

However, because of the distinctive nature of each club, one can perfectly be a member of both Soho House and The Residents Club®.
