# PAON Campaign and Strategy Library

**Status:** target product, editorial and technical design.

## The distinction

Klaviyo and similar platforms supply event-triggered messaging and flow
templates. PAON should not rebuild a generic email canvas first. It should
supply **complete menswear relationship programmes** that connect an idea to
customer eligibility, advisor actions, customer surfaces, appointments,
wardrobe work, products, services, timing and measurable outcomes.

Klaviyo's flow library and RFM-triggered retention validate reusable,
event-driven programmes. Sources:
[Klaviyo winback flows](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002775192)
and [RFM retention flows](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/25408596027547).

## Campaign package

Every library campaign is a versioned package containing:

- strategic premise and customer value;
- eligible verticals, seasons and countries/languages;
- audience rule template and minimum evidence;
- exclusions, contact pressure and cooldown;
- preparation timeline;
- advisor/manager/store missions;
- customer portal cards;
- approved email/SMS/message drafts;
- editorial/photo/video asset slots;
- product/knowledge/wardrobe requirements;
- appointment/event/service configuration;
- offers/rewards as optional provider-neutral hooks;
- compliance/brand approval state;
- outcome model and experiment;
- setup checklist and staff rehearsal;
- clone/localize/publish/archive lifecycle.

The retailer activates a copy pinned to a library version. PAON updates do not
silently change a live campaign.

## Deployment wizard

1. Preview the customer and employee experience.
2. Map retailer catalogue, services, branches, advisors and dates.
3. Show audience size and **why** each sample customer qualifies.
4. Identify missing assets/data/stock/capacity.
5. Choose channels and contact-pressure policy.
6. Assign preparation tasks.
7. Run a test customer and internal rehearsal.
8. Schedule or activate.
9. Monitor exceptions and outcome, not only sends/clicks.

The owner can turn on a campaign in minutes only when prerequisites are truly
ready. Otherwise PAON explains the three things preventing launch.

## Foundational campaigns

### Seven-Day Wardrobe

Promise: the advisor builds a complete week using owned pieces plus a small,
high-leverage roadmap.

Flow:

- customer selects a representative week/occasions;
- wardrobe engine proposes seven editable outfits;
- advisor reviews gaps and alternatives;
- customer receives one day at a time in MorningRoutine;
- missing piece candidates lead to save, remote proposal or appointment;
- wear feedback improves the wardrobe model.

Outcome: wardrobe activation, appointments, incremental additions and
customer-perceived usefulness—not seven emails.

### The Romantic Overcoat

Creative: early-evening city image of a man placing his overcoat over his
date's shoulders.

Timeline:

- early autumn: editorial story on why the overcoat is the most romantic
  wardrobe piece;
- customer sees owned-overcoat care/fit status and alternatives;
- advisor sees customers with formal evening occasions and no suitable coat;
- appointment/order timing respects production lead time;
- post-purchase campaign builds evening outfits around the coat.

### Valentine Rescue

Start months earlier with restaurant/experience partnerships and reservations,
not five days before with product spam.

- October/November: secure inventory of partner reservations/experiences;
- January: customer portal planning card and discreet save;
- two weeks before: advisor receives evidence-based customer prompts;
- five days before: eligible opted-in clients can receive a concise planning
  check;
- optional reservation, flowers, chauffeur, gift, wardrobe and alteration
  modules;
- customer controls surprise/privacy visibility.

PAON's value is operational pre-positioning. It must never invent an
anniversary or reveal a surprise to a shared account.

### Coming-of-Age Suit

Patek-inspired intergenerational milestone without copying its trade dress:

- parent/customer declares a coming-of-age/graduation milestone;
- story explains why a first serious suit becomes a long-lived object;
- appointment flow includes mentor/parent involvement;
- garment record captures story and care;
- future alteration/service and optional transfer/history live in wardrobe.

### Mother's Day / Father's Day

Not merely a discount:

- adult child/partner can contribute style context or a private note;
- remote gift consultation and appointment;
- experience/service alternatives when size is unknown;
- gift recipient enters their own profile and consent;
- post-gift onboarding becomes the relationship.

### Race Sunday / Monaco / annual events

Annual events become customer moments only when declared, attended previously
or inferred with sufficient cited evidence. Campaign packages include dress
code, travel/weather, production deadline, packing capsule and advisor timing.

### Honeymoon Phase

The order-to-delivery period is a relationship runway:

- transparent order tracker;
- appointment and collection planning;
- educational updates about garment/atelier;
- pre-delivery wardrobe preparation;
- social pickup option;
- controlled add-on suggestions;
- one-click reorder/offer only where provider eligibility exists;
- after delivery: care, outfit and feedback;
- weekly micro-capsules only with strong frequency controls.

The campaign exploits momentum by increasing usefulness and anticipation, not
by fabricating scarcity.

### Referral / MunroMentions

The customer sends a personal introduction or curated item/story to a friend.
The friend lands on a permissioned, retailer-branded context and chooses
whether to engage. Attribution and reward remain transparent.

### Dating / Single Again

Potentially powerful, potentially insulting. Never infer divorce or
relationship status from browsing. The customer or advisor must explicitly
record the context with appropriate visibility. Offer wardrobe confidence,
portrait session, grooming/venue partners and advisor appointment without
predatory copy.

## Customer surfaces

A campaign can render:

- MorningRoutine card;
- For You module;
- wardrobe challenge/plan;
- appointment or event;
- editorial story;
- personalized checklist;
- remote proposal/cart;
- referral;
- concierge action.

Email/SMS are delivery channels, not the campaign itself.

## Advisor and manager surfaces

Advisor:

- today/preparation queue;
- customer-specific why-now;
- approved message draft and edit;
- inventory/lead-time truth;
- promises and outcome capture.

Manager:

- launch readiness;
- assignment/coverage;
- completion and exception;
- outcome funnel;
- data/contact quality;
- branch comparison normalized for opportunity.

## Intelligence

Campaign audience uses deterministic eligibility over approved facts/events.
AI may:

- localize approved copy;
- draft a personalized message from eligible evidence;
- choose among approved content variants;
- summarize outcomes and objections.

AI may not invent dates, relationships, attendance, measurements, stock,
benefits or availability.

## Campaign outcome model

Track:

- eligible and suppressed;
- prepared/reached/responded;
- appointment/proposal/service/referral;
- conversion and contribution;
- unsubscribe/negative feedback;
- kept promises and fulfillment;
- customer usefulness feedback;
- advisor time and operational cost;
- correction/false-positive.

Use holdouts or staggered activation where the retailer accepts it. Attribution
is a contribution model, not a guarantee that a message caused a sale.
