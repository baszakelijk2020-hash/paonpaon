# PAON Knowledge, Consultancy, Training, and Media

**Status:** target editorial/product operating model.

## Purpose

PAON should make the retailer look and operate more intelligent. The highest
tier is not justified by hiding a PDF library behind a paywall. It is justified
when strategic thinking becomes reusable content, staff capability, customer
education and executable workflow.

## Four libraries

### Customer knowledge

Approved, neutral or retailer-local explanations:

- fused, half-canvas, full-canvas and handmade construction;
- fabric, weight, weave and season;
- fit, proportion and design;
- wedding/occasion planning;
- garment care and longevity;
- wardrobe planning and outfit logic.

Content connects to accepted catalogue metadata and actual garments. It must
not invent product composition or construction.

### Staff academy

Made-to-Munro-style learning:

- discovery and selling ceremony;
- measuring, fitting and design proficiency;
- fabric/product knowledge;
- objection and outcome practice;
- clienteling and data quality;
- service recovery;
- corporate-fashion programme;
- manager coaching.

Objects include lesson, scenario, roleplay, rubric, observation and
certification. Knowledge is delivered in context before a task, not only as a
course catalogue.

### Owner consultancy

Articles and tools demonstrating PAON's thinking:

- choreographed store layers/zones;
- preferred tailoring and HighMaintenance;
- customer journey and calendar;
- seven-day wardrobe;
- inventory/customer/workforce operating model;
- campaign economics;
- tender strategy;
- branch and advisor discipline;
- retailer positioning and expansion.

An article should lead into an audit, template, campaign or workflow. This is
how "consultancy" creates behavior rather than decoration.

### Lifestyle media

Licensed/approved articles, retailer-local stories, cultural partnerships and
campaign narratives that bring customers back between purchases. Each object
has rights/territory/expiry/attribution/commercial metadata and activation
surfaces.

## Content object

Canonical content records:

- type, audience and journey;
- title, summary and structured body;
- concepts/products/services/campaign relations;
- evidence/source and editorial author;
- image/video/assets;
- locale and translation relationship;
- rights/territory/expiry;
- review/approval and published version;
- safety/commercial classification;
- retailer overrides for presentation/pin/hide;
- CTA/action definition;
- outcome analytics.

AI drafts or localizes candidates. A human review publishes. Existing
knowledge precedence and accepted-metadata gates remain authoritative.

## Guided MTM tiers

The customer's choices should be packaged into coherent house programmes
rather than exposed as a thousand-option configurator:

- **Fused:** intentional accessible foundation, limited construction/options.
- **Half Canvas:** better structure and longevity with curated choice.
- **Full Canvas:** full premium construction and deeper personalization.
- **Handmade / Atelier:** intensive fit/design strategy and highest craft.

Names and exact benefits are retailer-configurable, but the system preserves
the capability/price/lead-time facts. Packages are versioned matrices of
permitted/default/recommended options. A retailer can also offer personality
edits such as Perfectionist or Specialist, mapped to the same canonical
specification rather than a separate garment type.

The customer begins with desired outcome/occasion and receives two or three
coherent paths. Experts can expand. This reduces choice paralysis without
pretending there is no choice.

## MunroMentor

Roleplay engine:

- scenario context and customer persona;
- permitted facts and objections;
- voice/text conversation;
- rubric for discovery, clarity, judgment and promise;
- cited moments from transcript;
- manager review and coaching action;
- reattempt and proficiency trend.

The model cannot grade facts it was not given. Evaluate behavior and outcome,
not accent, verbosity or similarity to one "perfect" script.

## DailyBriefing knowledge

Select one short, job-relevant object based on:

- appointments/products/services today;
- campaign/store mission;
- employee certification gap;
- recent customer objection;
- stock/production issue.

The employee can use it immediately and record whether it helped. This closes
the learning-to-outcome loop.

## Future proprietary products

Maintain a separately marked future-incubation register for white-label
products:

- garment/messenger bags;
- hangers, packaging and presentation;
- personal care/grooming;
- eyewear and accessories;
- store fixtures and measurement tools.

An idea graduates only after retailer demand, margin, supplier/quality,
inventory risk, claims, minimum order and channel conflict are assessed. It
enters MunroMerchant as a B2B listing, not the customer knowledge graph.
