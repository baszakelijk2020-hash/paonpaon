# Founder Intent and Platform Reset — 2026-08-01

**Status:** dated audit and rationale. It is not a queue or product authority.
Durable conclusions are incorporated into `NORTH_STAR.md`, `VISION.md`,
`PRODUCT.md`, ADR-069/070 and the control gate at the top of `PHASE.md`.

## Founder clarification — 2026-08-02

The Atelier Munro/Faden material is not a literal PAON specification. It is a
founder brain dump and design-research corpus from which PAON should extract,
combine, challenge and improve the strongest jobs, interactions and business
ideas. The initial reset below correctly found security, connected-product and
prioritization problems, but overcorrected by framing the Golden Relationship
Journey as the practical product boundary and later families as a mostly
optional portfolio.

ADR-070 corrects that interpretation. The complete modular PAON platform is
the committed destination. The Golden Relationship Journey is its first
demonstrator and shared spine; it does not limit the operations, enterprise,
vertical and ecosystem modules that will be built. Live money, data and
partner activation remains risk-gated while safe technical delivery proceeds.

### Correction: strategic corpus versus specified tools

The clarification above was itself too broad. The founder does not require
PAON to copy Atelier Munro's company, catalogue or every Nebelspiegel venture,
but does require the concrete tools he explicitly selected to be built as they
look and behave. Pag1's specified tools, pag2's groom/best-men fitting planner,
pag3's Preferred Tailoring/HighMaintenance workflow, and visually referenced
tools in the founder brief are exact experience contracts under ADR-052/071.
They must be connected to real PAON data and workflows, not translated into
generic screens. This correction governs the audit rows below.

## Scope and method

This audit compared:

- the original founder Faden / Mark-II brain dump;
- the complete live footer chapter set under `nebelspiegel.com/phone/`;
- committed `downloaded_pages/pag1.html`, `pag2.html` and `pag3.html`;
- the founder brief, north star, product, vision pillars, expanded programme,
  target architecture, competitive ledger, decisions and active queue;
- current app routes, domain packages, repositories and migrations;
- the Hyperagent commit range and its stated evidence.

The live chapters were inspected as rendered pages, including scroll pacing,
full-bleed/video openings, wardrobe rails, product composition and chapter
transitions. Text extraction was used to trace requirements, not as a
substitute for the visual pass.

## Executive verdict

The handoffs did not waste the founder's intent. They captured an unusual
amount of it and built real technical foundations. The principal failure is
**hierarchy, not comprehension**: almost every compelling idea was promoted
into an active platform capability, stage or schema before PAON had proven one
complete retailer relationship in production.

The result is technically ambitious and often thoughtful, but commercially
and experientially inverted:

- 154 page/route files, 143 domain source files, 84 repositories and 146
  migrations coexist with no established clean customer e2e baseline;
- the build models workforce, POS, inventory, corporate fashion, campaigns,
  services, fit, network commerce and later vertical packs while the primary
  role journeys remain fragmented;
- many source concepts were captured as nouns and tables, while the founder's
  strongest contribution—the orchestration of attention, desire, service and
  aftercare—was only partially translated into product interaction;
- completion evidence rewarded connected scaffolding more than market proof,
  onboarding readiness or one unmistakably excellent client outcome.

The corrective move is not to delete the ambition. It is to organize it as
one entitlement-controlled modular platform, sequenced by technical
dependencies and connected proof rather than by an ever-growing list of
isolated features.

## What the source material is really saying

The Mark-II work is not one software specification. It combines five types of
material:

1. **A service operating philosophy:** institutionalize exceptional advisory
   skill, fitting discipline, preparation and follow-through.
2. **Product interaction inventions:** wardrobe rails, composed outfits,
   TableService, MorningRoutine, post-order Honeymoon, short guided choices,
   customer-visible context and progress.
3. **A brand and demand strategy:** campaigns, cultural moments, events,
   Masterpieces, editorial and new occasions to dress.
4. **Operational systems:** analytics, training, production/fit evidence,
   inventory, corporate wardrobe operations and partner services.
5. **A platform expansion field:** marketplace, publisher, advertising,
   rewards, clubs, category expansion and supplier/retailer tools, mixed with
   some physical-property concepts that are not software requirements.

Treating all five as one undifferentiated application backlog is the source of
the current sprawl. PAON should translate them into coherent module families,
reuse one platform kernel and distinguish technical delivery from external
commercial activation. The source establishes intent; it does not dictate
PAON's names, screen structure or final solution.

## Visual and interaction findings

The source corpus uses a consistent experiential grammar:

- chapters open with black space, full-bleed moving imagery or live location
  context before explanation;
- narrow high-contrast editorial panels control pace and reveal;
- the wardrobe uses stacked horizontal product rails with a single luminous
  active choice rather than a conventional grid;
- MorningRoutine composes a person and complete look, then exposes small
  item-level include controls and one action;
- maps, weather, time, live video and event context make advice feel situated;
- story, product, service and transaction appear in one scroll narrative;
- the UI repeatedly reduces decisions to a short menu, a swipe or one tap.

Most PAON docs protected only a narrow set of verbatim ports, while later
handoffs converted other explicitly selected tools into generic reusable
primitives. That is the wrong default for designated tools. Preserve their
source composition and behavior, adding only narrow data/action and required
accessibility/security seams. Reusable `ChapterReveal`, `ContextStage`,
`WardrobeRail`, `ComposedLook`, `JourneyTimeline`, `ShortMenu` and
`EvidenceWhy` primitives remain appropriate for new or non-designated PAON
surfaces. Copying the black gradient alone would miss the point; replacing the
tool with a neat design-system card grid misses it too.

## Technical architecture verdict

### Worth keeping

- strict domain/repository/app separation;
- multi-tenant/RLS intent and forward migrations;
- explicit source authority, external identity and reconciliation;
- provenance classes, consent, evidence citations and deterministic-first
  intelligence;
- wardrobe, garment, fit, campaign and service primitives;
- connected-proof discipline and migration rehearsal;
- overlay/co-managed/full-authority modes.

### Must be corrected before more breadth

- cross-tenant inventory ledger references can mutate another tenant's stock;
- a public `SECURITY DEFINER` location helper can create a location for an
  arbitrary retailer;
- POS completion and return flows are not atomic;
- the Faden adapter invents a signing scheme and headers not established by
  Faden's public contract;
- live integration proof is fixture/setup dependent and did not produce the
  claimed 67 executed assertions in the audited environment;
- customer e2e remains 15/29; the same 14 journeys also failed before the
  Hyperagent range, so the debt predates that work but remains real;
- the active Supabase/Vercel/production relationship is not established and
  test suites write real rows;
- clean-database migration success is not proof of production upgrade safety;
- navigation and role homes expose organisational breadth instead of the next
  job.

## Faden boundary

Faden publicly presents a tailoring CRM, garment configurator, production
board, workroom portal, versioned measurements, payments, inventory, branded
client portal, Shopify sync, read-only REST API and webhooks. That makes it a
substantial operational system, not a minor production endpoint.

PAON should therefore:

- integrate Faden as an authoritative MTM/production system when a retailer
  uses it;
- ingest only fields and events supported by a current written contract;
- deep-link unsupported actions and reconcile completion;
- own the broader relationship, wardrobe, client-facing continuity,
  campaigns and aftercare layer;
- compete with or replace Faden only after PAON has a validated customer
  segment whose buying reason requires that ownership.

No route should describe a fixture HMAC convention as a live Faden webhook
contract.

## Assessment of the Hyperagent spend

The work is materially useful, not a sham and not remotely equivalent to
throwing the day away. It produced broad domain coverage, migrations,
repositories, tests, operational evidence and several difficult cross-system
foundations. A smaller model or casual coding session would not have produced
the same volume.

It was also not an economically outstanding use of roughly $550 / 1.02B
tokens. The marginal token spend went into breadth, repeated verification,
large documentation/evidence machinery and speculative platform surface area
before security, environment truth, primary e2e and market proof were closed.
The correct rating is **valuable middle, with serious overproduction**.

A fair qualitative score:

- intent capture: 8/10;
- architecture and domain thinking: 7.5/10;
- raw implementation output: 8/10;
- connected product completion: 5/10;
- commercial prioritization: 3.5/10;
- security/operational readiness: 4/10;
- visual translation of founder intent: 4.5/10;
- value for the stated inference cost: 4/10.

The value is recovered by consolidating the good primitives into a coherent
modular programme, proving complete slices and continuing toward the full
destination instead of either following the old queue by inertia or shrinking
PAON to its first launch journey.

## Corrected strategic reset

The new order is:

1. establish environment truth and contain security/data risks;
2. implement the module/entitlement kernel and one canonical House dataset;
3. restore deployed customer and retailer journeys to a reliable baseline;
4. prove the shared intelligence spine through one exceptional multi-role
   relationship journey;
5. realize Wardrobe/Styling, Commerce/Growth and Garment/Service Operations as
   complete configurable modules;
6. realize Retail Operations with explicit overlay/co-managed/full authority;
7. realize Enterprise and Vertical Solutions as reusable packaged journeys;
8. realize Network and Ecosystem infrastructure, gating only live partner,
   money, advertising and sensitive-data activation on its actual risks;
9. validate all chapters continuously through real onboarding, usage,
   connected proof, plan packaging and measurable retailer/client outcomes.

This preserves the scale of the founder's ambition while making it legible,
testable, technically achievable and sellable.
