## Coverage gaps (explicitly not tested this pass, not silently omitted)

- No admin-app screens beyond login/dashboard/retailer-network/onboarding/daily-briefing were
  opened (e.g. any billing/financial oversight, platform-level staff invite screens).
- No retailer-app screens for `production_staff`/`read_only` roles.
- No mobile coverage at all for: retail-worker (invalidated before mobile pass), workshop
  (invalidated before mobile pass), customer's 5 authenticated routes beyond login.
- No error-state screens (network failure, 500, session-expired) were deliberately triggered and
  screenshotted on any app this pass — the negative-testing prompts largely asked for this and
  it was inconsistently executed (retail-worker's lazy-pass did cover some of this at the
  interaction level — back/forward, refresh, concurrent-tab — but not visual error-state capture).
- No dedicated loading-state screenshots were captured on any app.
