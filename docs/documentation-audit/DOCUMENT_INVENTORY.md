# Document Inventory

Audit-only deliverable. Produced by reading every maintained Markdown document
in the PAON repository (root, `docs/`, `.claude/agents`, `.claude/prompts`)
plus code, migrations and git history as corroborating evidence. Excludes
`.claude/worktrees/**`, which are stale git-worktree checkouts (three full
duplicate copies of the repo from prior parallel-agent sessions, most
recently touched under branches `agent-a0fbd32efe6e81f8c`,
`agent-a444e6e4198a3f019`, `agent-a665daf8889fdfd73`) — not canonical
documentation. **Recommended disposition for the worktrees themselves: they
are not documents and are out of this audit's scope, but they are stale
working trees that should be reviewed for removal (`git worktree remove`)
once any in-flight lane work inside them is confirmed merged or abandoned —
that is a repository-hygiene action, not a documentation action, and is
called out here only because it explains the 437-vs-113 Markdown-file
discrepancy a naive `find` turns up.**

Total maintained documents inventoried: **112** (3 root, 30 `docs/` root, 17
`docs/ai_snapshot/`, 10 `docs/archive/`, 12 `docs/audits/`, 3
`docs/evidence/`, 2 `docs/runbooks/`, 31 `docs/vision/`, 3 `.claude/agents/`,
1 `.claude/prompts/`).

Disposition vocabulary used below (per the audit brief): **keep
authoritative** · **keep operational** · **merge into another document** ·
**split into multiple documents** · **replace with a new canonical
document** · **archive as historical** · **retain as evidence** · **retire
only after founder approval**.

No file may be marked for retirement here — that requires explicit founder
sign-off, tracked in `FOUNDER_QUESTIONS.md`. "Archive as historical" means
"move to `docs/archive/` in the migration plan," not "delete."
