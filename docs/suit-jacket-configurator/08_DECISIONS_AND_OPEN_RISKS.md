## Roadmap

Ground-zero reconciliation is complete and the authorization gate is released
for **Lab Phase 1 only** (D-14). The full plan, workstreams W1–W6, sequencing
and exit criteria are in chapter 10. In outline:

1. **Lab Phase 1 — prove the comparison reads.** W1 dependency and pin; W2
   family and assembly generator; W3 headless simulation and bake, emitting
   tier 1 and tier 2 together; W4 manifest and CI gates; W5 the three delivery
   tiers; W6 the observer study. Sequenced smallest-first: one family and two
   interchangeable assemblies settle R-14 before any breadth is authored.
2. **Lab Phase 2 — breadth**, only if Phase 1's exit criteria pass: more
   assemblies and families, then isolated Studio-side read-only composition
   behind existing consent and entitlement gates. Needs separate authorization.
3. **Lab Phase 3 — commercial contracts**, only after proposal/MTM contracts
   are accepted: snapshots, compatibility, price and advisor continuation.
   Never inferred from this lab. Needs separate authorization.

Calibration is not on this roadmap as a scheduled item. It is gated on
physical swatch evidence that PAON does not have and will not manufacture a
founder task to obtain (R-02).
